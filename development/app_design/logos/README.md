# Logo designs

The **blade ring** is the approved logo used in the optimization app. All three original logo families are preserved here with their SVG content unchanged.

Open the [gallery](index.html) or [PNG comparison](logo_studies.png) to compare each mark on light and dark surfaces at 16, 24 and 48 px.

The PNG is preserved from the original logo study and still contains its historical proposal labels. The gallery records the current approval status.
