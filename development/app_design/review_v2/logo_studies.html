Suggested default: blade ring. Alternatives: axial cascade or TD monogram. Review the 24 px mark in the actual app header before selecting a direction. The wordmark is live text and the icons are scalable SVGs, so neither relies on a downloaded font or a raster asset. The review app contains the editable decision and feedback fields.
