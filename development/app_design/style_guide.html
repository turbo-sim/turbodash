02 / Typography

Technical clarity, without decoration.

Use the system sans-serif stack led by Segoe UI. Labels stay compact, numbers align, and units remain attached to their quantities.

Spacing carries the hierarchy

Short labels and quiet borders group controls. A bold section title and numbered badge orient the reader without repeated banners or oversized cards.

03 / Identity and symbols

A compact mark. A live wordmark.

The approved blade ring uses three swept blades, a circular casing, and one blue accent. The wordmark stays lowercase text. Geometry and reconstruction instructions are in iconography.md.

The dark logo accent is #81B3FF; the dark interface accent is #88B7FF. Header logos follow the app theme. Favicons follow the browser's preferred color scheme. Keep those behaviors distinct.

04 / Workbench anatomy

Persistent context, progressive detail.

Separate workspace navigation from parameter editing and results. Keep design values mounted when sections close or the stage changes. Full behavior is recorded in components-and-layout.md.
